About this app

VitaGauge is developed by zhichao wei for iPhone. It organizes authorized Apple Health records and supports personal goals and general information. Availability of the upcoming Pro features depends on the App Store release.

Purchases and restoration

A non-consumable, one-time purchase with no automatic renewal. Restore using the original Apple Account. It does not include an API key, model credits or provider fees. Free features remain available without buying. Purchase and refunds are managed by Apple. Family Sharing is not enabled for this Pro product. Pending approval is not a completed purchase.

Your online provider

You choose and pay your own provider. Never share API keys in support emails. Provider availability, fees and data practices follow its terms. Changing recipient requires new consent. The app cannot delete information already received by a provider.

Health and data limits

VitaGauge organizes health records for everyday self-management. It is not a medical device and does not provide diagnoses, prescriptions or emergency monitoring. Consult a qualified professional for health concerns. Missing measurements are not zero, and correlations do not establish causation. This app is not intended for children under 13.

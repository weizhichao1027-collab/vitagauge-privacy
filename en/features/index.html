The shipping interface

Know the limits before you buy

The offline assistant uses rules, built-in information and deterministic math. It supports recent 1 to 30 day windows and explicit dates. Longer windows, date comparisons and correlations need Pro. Missing data is not zero. Correlation is not causation. Both online modes need Pro, your key and recipient consent. AI replies do not replace care.

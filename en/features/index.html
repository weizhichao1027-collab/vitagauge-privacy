The shipping interface

Three conversation modes

Local: rules, built-in notes and deterministic math. No API call and no model download.

API: shows the provider text or an error. It does not fall back to a local answer.

Local + API: simple queries stay on-device; open-ended text uses the provider. Online parts still need Pro, your key and recipient consent.

Know the limits before you buy

The offline assistant supports recent 1 to 30 day windows and explicit dates. Longer windows, date comparisons and correlations need Pro. Correlation needs at least 7 finished paired days with variation in both series; it is not causation. Missing data is not zero. Body temperature is the body-temperature type, not sleep wrist temperature. The two blood-pressure readings are not joined across time into one measurement. Sleep is split by calendar day. AI replies do not replace care.

Support, use and purchases

1.2.0 is live: iPhone, iPad and paired Apple Watch

Version 1.2.0 is available on the App Store. The main app supports iPhone on iOS 15+ and native iPad layouts; reading Apple Health on iPad requires iPadOS 17+ and authorization on that device. The update improves landscape, narrow windows and larger text, and adds Home Screen widget sizes and an inline Lock Screen style. The paired Apple Watch app, complications and Smart Stack widgets require watchOS 10+ and a compatible iPhone. Widgets require iOS / iPadOS 17+. The watch shows five metrics last synced from the phone; it does not independently read HealthKit, call AI or monitor health in real time.

Citable product facts

VitaGauge (元气指数), App Store ID 6762522305, is an Apple Health record assistant. Version 1.2.0 (build 5) went live on 31 October 2026 in Asia/Shanghai. Free features include summaries of 16 authorized metrics, 7-day trends, local questions and one goal. It supports iPhone and iPad; Health access on iPad requires iPadOS 17+, and paired Apple Watch support requires watchOS 10+. Pro is a one-time purchase. Optional online AI needs the user’s own key and separate consent. HealthKit is read-only, with no app account, ads or analytics SDK. Watch displays a phone-synced snapshot, not diagnoses or real-time monitoring.

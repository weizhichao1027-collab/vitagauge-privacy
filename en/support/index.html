Frequently asked questions

How do I connect Apple Health?

Tap Connect Apple Health on first launch and choose the types to allow in the system sheet. You can also use Settings → Health data & permissions later. VitaGauge reads records; it does not write to HealthKit.

Why are some metrics blank?

Check that Apple Health has records for that date, review read permissions, then refresh the summary. Some metrics require Apple Watch, a compatible device or another source. A blank result is not zero and cannot distinguish missing data from denied access.

What works without an API key?

Summaries, 7-day trends, basic local queries, general lifestyle information and one goal. Try “steps today”, “steps daily for the last 7 days” or “how to add more activity”. Offline mode requires no network or model download. Pro local statistics do not require an API either.

Why does the assistant ask for a date?

Expressions such as “last night” or “last week” do not reliably match the daily aggregation. Use yesterday, the last 7 days, or an explicit date such as 2026-09-01. Missing or ambiguous records are not invented.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. It is a one-time purchase. The China launch price is CNY 38; Apple shows your actual local price before purchase. No monthly or yearly renewal. Your online AI provider may charge separately for use of your API key.

How do I restore on a new phone?

Sign in with the original Apple Account and open Settings → VitaGauge Pro → Restore purchases. Restoring Pro does not restore chats, goals or API credentials: these sensitive local files are excluded from app backups.

I paid, but Pro is still locked.

Check whether Apple completed the transaction or is waiting for approval. Connect to the internet and restore purchases. If the issue persists, contact support with a redacted error message. Request refunds through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I connect an online provider?

Open Settings → Connect online AI, select a provider and follow its official key guide. Paste your key, then verify and save. Verification sends only fixed test text and may incur provider fees. In version 1.1.0, after verifying and saving, choose Local, API or Local + API; you can also switch in the assistant’s ⋯ → Conversation mode menu. API displays provider-generated replies and shows an error on failure. Local + API processes simple queries on-device and uses online AI for open-ended explanations. Local sends no online conversation requests and keeps the saved key. Online use requires Pro and separate data consent before the first send to that recipient.

Why does voice input fail?

Check microphone and speech permissions and whether your device and language support on-device recognition. The app does not fall back to cloud speech; use text input when local recognition is unavailable. Voice improvements in version 1.1.0: tap to start and tap again to finish, or hold to speak and release to finish. Slide up or choose Cancel to discard this recording. Transcribed text goes into the editor for review before you send. After granting permissions, you may need to tap the microphone again. At the 55-second limit, recording ends and recognized text is retained. If the input changes, check the microphone and retry.

How do I delete records and keys?

Clear conversations from the assistant menu, delete goals on the goals page, and clear AI configuration to remove the Keychain credential. Clear the key before uninstalling. Local deletion does not erase Apple Health originals or data a provider already received.

Can VitaGauge tell me if I am ill?

No. It does not diagnose, recommend medication doses or monitor emergencies. Built-in lifestyle information is not personalized treatment. Consult a clinician for symptoms and local emergency services for emergencies.

Which queries need Pro?

Ordinary windows of up to 7 days are free, including the last 2 days. Comparing two selected dates, windows longer than 7 days, correlations and multiple goals require Pro. If the product or price is unavailable, purchase is disabled; retry or restore is available.

HOME AND LOCK SCREEN WIDGETS

On iOS 17 or later, add widgets for your most recently synced health summary and goals. Open the app to sync. Widgets show the sync time and let you hide health values; they do not provide real-time monitoring.

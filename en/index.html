01Scope and developer

This policy covers VitaGauge (元气指数, “the app”) and this privacy website. The developer provides privacy and user support through the email below. The app requires no developer account and operates no server that relays health conversations to AI services.

02Information used by the app

Apple Health: After you grant system permission, the app may read steps, walking and running distance, active energy, heart rate, resting heart rate, blood oxygen, sleep, weight, workouts, heart rate variability, respiratory rate, cardio fitness, body fat percentage, systolic and diastolic blood pressure, and body temperature. These support on-device summaries, trends and goal reviews. The app reads HealthKit data and does not write health samples. Original records remain managed by Apple Health.

Information you provide: Chat questions, goals and notes, optional nickname, gender and avatar, and your AI endpoint, model and API key. Avatars are used for local display and are not attached to AI requests.

Voice input: Microphone and speech permissions are used when you start speech-to-text. Recognition runs on-device for supported devices and languages, without a cloud-recognition fallback. In version 1.1.0, recognized text first appears in the editor for your review; it follows the chat rules when you send it.

03When information goes to third-party AI

After you choose an AI service (and select API chat in version 1.1.0), separately consent to its destination and actively send a question, the current question and a length-limited recent conversation are sent directly from your device to your configured endpoint over HTTPS. Your API key authenticates that request.

New health context is selected for the question: usually the last 7 days, or up to 30 days for monthly trends. Explicit historical dates may add selected metric summaries for those dates. Goal or profile text is included only when relevant to the question. Greetings and app-usage questions do not attach new health summaries by default.

Local queries reuse your existing system Health permissions without asking again for every question. You can inspect data usage in the assistant. The first send to your configured third-party AI requires separate consent. Consent is saved on this device and reused for the same recipient; you can revoke it in Settings. Recent messages, including health information already in them, may still be sent with online questions. Clear the conversation first to remove that history. Minute-by-minute raw samples, avatars and internal model reasoning are not sent as attachments.

Changing the destination requires renewed consent. Changing the address or protocol requires a newly entered key. Revoking consent cancels the current request and prevents new sends, but cannot recall information already delivered to the recipient.

Local queries and built-in information run on the device without sending a model request. In version 1.1.0, Local chat sends no online conversation requests even when an API is configured. Online mode can discuss everyday habits; health figures are grounded in local calculations, and replies cannot replace medical care. Apple processes Pro purchases; verified StoreKit transactions determine access. We do not receive your card details.

04Third parties and purposes

The developer does not sell health information, use it for advertising, or include advertising trackers or third-party behavioral analytics SDKs in the app. Information supports the health display, goal management and answers you request.

Your chosen AI provider independently processes information it receives. Retention, model-training use, processing locations, fees and deletion depend on that provider’s terms and your account settings; processing may occur outside your region. Review the provider’s privacy policy before sending. You may leave AI disabled or revoke consent. This policy does not guarantee the privacy practices of arbitrary custom endpoints.

05Local storage, retention and safeguards

The app does not bulk-copy raw health samples into a separate health database. Goals, refresh logs, profile, avatar, AI configuration metadata and chats are stored in the app’s on-device storage. These sensitive files use iOS file protection and are excluded from system backups. Chats may contain health information within questions or answers.

Memory and disk share a bounded chat window: at most 100 recent messages, 200,000 UTF-8 bytes of message text in total, and 24,000 characters per message. Older messages may be removed sooner when a limit is reached. Model reasoning is not persisted. If you explicitly recover a damaged profile, a protected local copy of the damaged original is kept first.

API keys use iOS Keychain with unlocked-device access and a device-only setting; they are bound to their endpoint and protocol and are not synchronized through iCloud Keychain. Keys are not embedded in the app, logged, or sent to the developer’s servers. Your configured AI endpoint receives your key for authentication.

When the app enters the background, it covers sensitive content in the app switcher and cancels AI, voice and pending health queries. These safeguards reduce risk; no storage or transmission can be guaranteed absolutely secure.

06Choices, withdrawal and deletion

You can manage read permissions in Apple Health or iOS health privacy settings, revoke third-party AI consent or clear the AI configuration and key in the app, clear local chats in the assistant, delete goals, or edit your profile. Local health summaries and goals do not require third-party AI.

Uninstalling removes app sandbox files. Before uninstalling, use “Clear AI configuration” to delete the Keychain credential; do not rely on uninstalling to guarantee key deletion. Revoke or rotate the key with your AI provider if it must become invalid immediately. Local deletion does not delete original Apple Health records or information already received by a provider.

There is no developer account and no separate health export feature in the app. Use Apple Health for original health-data exports. Contact your chosen AI provider about information it has received. You may contact the developer below about access, correction or deletion of support correspondence held by the developer.

07This website and support contact

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages and has no sign-in, forms, analytics scripts or external fonts. The page does not request health information or API keys. GitHub may process IP addresses, browser information and access logs to operate its services; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

If you email the developer, your email address, message and attachments are received to respond and handle your request. Do not send API keys or unnecessary health information. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to handle requests and applicable retention obligations.

08Children and health information

The app is not intended for children under 13. Contact the developer if you believe a child has provided personal information to the developer.

The app provides everyday health information and AI reference content. It is not a medical device and does not replace professional diagnosis, prescriptions or treatment. Contact local emergency services for urgent medical situations.

09Policy changes

This policy is effective September 12, 2026. Updates will show a new date on this page. Material changes will be communicated in the app or App Store release notes. New processing that requires consent will seek it as applicable; continued use does not replace necessary explicit consent.
